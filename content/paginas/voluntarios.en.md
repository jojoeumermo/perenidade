We have no sponsor, no ads and no staff. Everything here was written, reviewed and organised by people who believe knowledge should belong to everyone. There are many ways to help, and none of them requires a degree.

## As a reviewer

This is the easiest way to start. While reading a lesson you may find:

- a typo or a spelling mistake;
- information that is wrong or out of date;
- a confusing explanation that could be clearer.

Every lesson and every library entry has a **Suggest a correction** link at the bottom. It opens the text for editing: you fix it and send the proposal. If you would rather just let us know, [open a suggestion](https://github.com/jojoeumermo/perenidade/issues/new?template=sugestao.yml) stating the page, the error and the correction.

New lessons carry the notice "awaiting community review". Reviewing one of them from start to finish, checking dates, sums and examples, is one of the most valuable contributions.

## As an educator

Can you explain something? Write a lesson. It can be in any field, from primary school subjects to political theory. Use the [Contribute](/contribuir) page, which builds the file in the right format.

Some guidance:

- Write for beginners. Short sentences, everyday examples.
- One subject per lesson. Three short lessons are better than one long one.
- The text must be yours. Nothing copied from books, handouts or websites.
- By submitting, you publish under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence, which guarantees your credit and the freedom to share.

## As a translator

The site exists in Portuguese, English, Spanish, Chinese and Russian, but most lessons are still only in Portuguese. If you are fluent in another language, translating a lesson opens the library to millions of people.

- Before you start, check that the lesson has not been translated already.
- Machine-only translations are not accepted. You may use tools for support, but the final text must be reviewed by someone fluent in the language.
- A translation is a file next to the original, with the language code in its name. For example, the English translation of `01-fracoes.md` is `01-fracoes.en.md`. The codes are `en`, `es`, `zh` and `ru`.

The translations of the site's interface were also made with machine support and need review by native speakers.

## As the organiser of a shelf or an author

Do you have a favourite subject or author? You can look after that shelf. An organiser's responsibilities are:

- keeping the texts free of errors;
- finding and suggesting free works for the library;
- reviewing the lessons and translations submitted in that area.

## As a curator of the library

The library only indexes: it keeps the title, the author, a synopsis and the link to the legitimate source. You can suggest works in the public domain or under an open licence through the [Contribute](/contribuir) page, and also help check that older links still work.

## As a developer or designer

The code is open and lives on [GitHub](https://github.com/jojoeumermo/perenidade). Fixes, accessibility improvements and new ideas are welcome. The site's look is intentionally simple and light, so that it works on old phones and weak connections.

## Spreading the word

Tell your colleagues, your union, your community course, your school about the library. A teacher can print a lesson and take it to class. A study group can use a whole track. That is what it is for.

## Mission board

What the library needs most right now:

- **Review the first lessons.** All of them are still waiting to be checked by a second person.
- **Translate** the lessons into English, Spanish, Chinese and Russian.
- **Write lessons** for the empty shelves: Portuguese, Geography, Sociology, Philosophy and Natural sciences.
- **History from below:** slave revolts, strikes and popular uprisings in your country.
- **Suggest free works** by Latin American, African and Asian authors.
- **Expand the Memorial** with names from your region.

Want to take on a mission? [Open a suggestion](https://github.com/jojoeumermo/perenidade/issues/new?template=sugestao.yml) saying which one, so that nobody does the same work twice.

## Everything here is free

The project charges nobody anything and is not for profit. The best contribution is your time and your knowledge.
