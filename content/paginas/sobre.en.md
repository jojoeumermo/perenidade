The Library of Alexandria did not vanish in a single fire. It withered through centuries of neglect, cuts and conflict, and what was lost was lost because it existed in only a few copies. Instituto Lúcia exists for the opposite: knowledge that is free, costs nothing and is copied by everyone.

The knowledge humanity has gathered was produced by generations of working women and men. It cannot be a commodity, nor a privilege of those who can pay. Here nobody pays, nobody needs an account to learn, and nobody makes a profit.

The name is a tribute: see the [dedication](/dedicatoria).

## Whose side we are on

This is a left-wing project, and it does not hide it. We stand with those who work, with those who fight for land, housing, wages and freedom, and with all peoples who resist exploitation and oppression.

That is why our History is told from the point of view of those who made it with their own hands. That is why there is a [Memorial](/memorial) here for those who fell in the struggle, an [Authors](/autores) section gathering revolutionary thought, a [Glossary](/glossario) of concepts and a [Timeline of struggles](/linha-do-tempo).

But the institute is not only about political theory. Anyone who wants to learn English, mathematics, music, computing, programming or a trade to earn a living finds the same care here. Education is a tool of emancipation in every field.

## How it works

- **Subjects** are divided into tracks, from primary school to higher education. Each lesson is short, ends with a quiz and shows the sources it relies on.
- **Video lessons** are short, up to 10 minutes, recorded by the educators themselves and reviewed like the written ones, with a transcript and adjustable speed.
- **The library** is an index: title, author, synopsis and a link to the legitimate source of the work. No third-party file is hosted here.
- **The practice tools** (tuner, code editor, periodic table and others) run on your own device.
- **The curators** read everything before it is published. Anyone can [contribute](/contribuir), with or without an account, and every lesson must cite at least one source.
- **The history** keeps every version. If something wrong is published, we simply go back to the previous version.

## Our principles, in one minute

1. Knowledge is free. Nothing here is for sale.
2. We do not sell your data. To study, we do not even need it.
3. No ads, no trackers, no rankings, no rush. Everyone learns at their own pace.
4. What is taught here has a source, and readers can check it.
5. We respect the work of those who write: only what is free to circulate comes in.

## Who keeps it going

The institute is kept going by volunteers: educators and secondary educators, curators, reviewers, translators, mediators, mentors, journalists, designers, marketing and developers, together with the coordination and vice-coordination. See how to take part in [Volunteering](/voluntarios). The cost of keeping the site online is paid by those who can and want to help: see [Support](/apoie). Donating is optional, and access is the same for everyone.

## Privacy

Studying requires no sign-up. The lessons you complete, the theme you choose, your reading notes and your drafts are stored only in your browser. In [My data](/meus-dados) you can download or erase everything.

An account is optional and is only for those who want to join the conversations or work as volunteers. In that case we keep your email and the name you choose to show; team members have a few more data, all listed in the [privacy policy](/privacidade). You can delete the account whenever you like, from the panel itself. The site uses no tracking cookies and no ads.

## Copyright

Only works in the public domain, under an open licence or written by the community itself come in here. Protected books appear only as bibliographic references, with a link to the publisher or to a library. If something is wrong, [report it](/denunciar): the item is taken down while it is reviewed.
