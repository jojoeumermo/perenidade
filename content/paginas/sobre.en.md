The Library of Alexandria did not vanish in a single fire. It withered over centuries of neglect, cuts and conflict, and what was lost was lost because it existed in too few copies. Projeto Lucia exists for the opposite: knowledge that is free, open and copied by everyone.

The knowledge humanity has accumulated was produced by generations of working women and men. It cannot be a commodity or a privilege of those who can pay. Here nobody pays, nobody needs an account to learn, and nobody profits.

## Whose side we are on

This is a left-wing project, and it does not hide it. We stand with those who work, with those who fight for land, housing, wages and freedom, and with all peoples who resist exploitation and oppression.

That is why our History shelf is told from the point of view of those who made history with their own hands. That is why you will find a [Memorial](/memorial) for those who fell in the struggle, an [Authors](/autores) section gathering revolutionary thought, a [Glossary](/glossario) of concepts and a [Timeline of struggles](/linha-do-tempo).

But the library is not only about political theory. Anyone who wants to learn English, mathematics, computing or programming will find the same care here. Education is a tool of emancipation in every field.

## How it works

- **Tracks and lessons** are texts written by volunteers and stored as plain files.
- **The library** is an index: title, author, synopsis and a link to the legitimate source of each work. No third-party file is hosted here.
- **Moderation** is public. Every contribution goes through the [moderation queue](/moderacao) before it is accepted.
- **The history** keeps every version. See the [history of the collection](/historico).
- **The code** is open. If this site ever goes down, anyone can copy it and bring it back.

## Our principles, in one minute

1. Knowledge is free. Nothing here is for sale.
2. We do not sell your data, because we do not have it.
3. No ads, no trackers, no rankings, no hurry. Everyone learns at their own pace.
4. Nobody owns the library. Whoever uses it can improve it.
5. We respect the work of those who write: only what is free to circulate comes in.

## Privacy

The site has no sign-up, uses no tracking cookies and collects no personal data. The lessons you complete, your chosen theme and your contribution drafts are stored only in your browser. In [My data](/meus-dados) you can download or erase everything. The only exception is the moderation queue, which queries GitHub directly from your browser to list open contributions.

## Copyright

Only works in the public domain, under an open licence or written by the community itself are accepted. If something is wrong, [report it](/denunciar): the item is taken down while it is reviewed.
