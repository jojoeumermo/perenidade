A fashion designer from Minas Gerais who took Brazilian fashion to international catwalks. When her son, Stuart Angel, was arrested and killed by the dictatorship in 1971 and his body disappeared, she turned fashion into a denunciation, with collections embroidered with angels, cannons and caged birds, and took the case to authorities abroad. She died on 14 April 1976 in a car crash that was caused deliberately, at the exit of the tunnel that now bears her name. In 1998 the Brazilian state acknowledged the dictatorship's responsibility.
