Philosopher, journalist and communist leader born in Sardinia. He studied how the powerful rule not only by force but also through consent built in schools, the press and culture, and so he argued that working people should form their own intellectuals. Imprisoned by Mussolini's fascist regime in 1926, he wrote the Prison Notebooks behind bars. He died on 27 April 1937, days after his sentence ended, his health destroyed by more than ten years in prison.
