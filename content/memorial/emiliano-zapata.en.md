A peasant from Morelos and a leader of the Mexican Revolution. With the Plan of Ayala in 1911, he demanded that land seized by the great estates be returned to the villages, under the slogan “Land and liberty”. He led the Liberation Army of the South and refused deals that left peasants without land. He was killed in an ambush set up by the government on 10 April 1919, at the Chinameca hacienda.
