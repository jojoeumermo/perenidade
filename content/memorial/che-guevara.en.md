An Argentine physician, he travelled across Latin America as a young man and saw the continent's poverty at close quarters. He joined the Cuban revolutionaries, was a commander of the guerrilla force that overthrew the dictatorship of Fulgencio Batista in 1959 and held posts in the new government. He left them to support liberation movements in the Congo and in Bolivia, where he was captured and executed on 9 October 1967. Around the world he became a symbol of solidarity among peoples.
