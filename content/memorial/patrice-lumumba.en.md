Leader of the Congo's struggle for independence from Belgian colonial rule and the country's first head of government, in 1960. On independence day, before the King of the Belgians, he recalled in a speech the humiliations and violence suffered by the Congolese people. A few months later he was deposed, arrested and handed over to his enemies, who shot him on 17 January 1961 with the participation of Belgian officers. In 2002 the Belgian government acknowledged its moral responsibility for the assassination.
