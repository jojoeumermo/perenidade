A leader of the Lenca indigenous people and founder of the Civic Council of Popular and Indigenous Organizations of Honduras. She organised the communities' resistance to the building of a hydroelectric dam on the Gualcarque River, sacred to the Lenca, and in 2015 received the Goldman Prize, the world's foremost award for defenders of the environment. She was shot dead at home in the early hours of 3 March 2016. Honduran courts convicted the gunmen and a former executive of the company in charge of the project.
