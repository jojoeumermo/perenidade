A physician and socialist politician, he was elected president of Chile in 1970 at the head of Popular Unity, proposing to reach socialism through the ballot box. His government nationalised copper, deepened the agrarian reform and guaranteed half a litre of milk a day to every child. On 11 September 1973 a military coup backed by the United States bombed the La Moneda Palace. Allende refused to resign, said farewell to the people by radio and died in the palace. A seventeen-year dictatorship began.
