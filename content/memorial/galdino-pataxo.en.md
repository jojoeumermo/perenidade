A leader of the Pataxó Hã-Hã-Hãe people of southern Bahia. He went to Brasília in April 1997 to defend the demarcation of his people's lands. In the early hours of 20 April, after the events of Indigenous Peoples' Day, he was sleeping at a bus stop when five young men doused him in alcohol and set him on fire. He died in hospital, with almost his whole body burned. The crime shocked the country and became a symbol of violence against Indigenous peoples.
