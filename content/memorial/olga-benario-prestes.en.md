A communist activist born in Munich to a Jewish family. She came to Brazil in 1935 with her partner Luís Carlos Prestes and was arrested in 1936, after the uprising of November 1935. Pregnant, she was handed over by Getúlio Vargas's government to Nazi Germany, despite protests. Her daughter, Anita Leocádia, was born in prison and was handed to her paternal grandmother after an international campaign. Olga was murdered in a gas chamber in Bernburg in 1942.
