A Palestinian journalist born in Jerusalem, for 25 years she was the voice that told the Arab world about daily life under occupation. On 11 May 2022 she was killed by a shot to the head while covering an Israeli army operation in the Jenin refugee camp; she was wearing a helmet and a vest marked "press". The United Nations human rights office concluded that the shots came from Israeli forces. The Israeli army admitted there was a "high possibility" that she had been hit by its soldiers, unintentionally.
