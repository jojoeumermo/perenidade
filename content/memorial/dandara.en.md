Tradition holds that she was a warrior of the Quilombo dos Palmares and the partner of Zumbi, that she opposed the peace agreement with the colonial government and that she died in 1694, when the capital of the quilombo was destroyed. The documents of the time barely record the women of Palmares, and there are no written records about her. Today her name stands for all the Black women who fought against slavery and whom official history erased. Since 2019 it has been inscribed in Brazil's Book of Heroes and Heroines of the Fatherland.
