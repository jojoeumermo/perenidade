Manoel Henrique Pereira, a capoeira player from the Recôncavo region of Bahia, lived at a time when capoeira was a crime in Brazil. A worker on cargo boats and sugar mills, he became known for standing up to the police and to the overseers who mistreated workers. Accounts of his life mix documents and legend: it was said that no blade could harm him. He died in July 1924 at the charity hospital of Santo Amaro, after being stabbed in an ambush. He is remembered in capoeira circles and songs as one of the greatest names of the art.
