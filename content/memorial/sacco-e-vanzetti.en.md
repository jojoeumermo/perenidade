Italian immigrant workers and anarchists: Sacco worked in a shoe factory, Vanzetti sold fish. Arrested in 1920 and accused of a robbery in which two men were killed, they were convicted in a trial marked by prejudice against immigrants and against their ideas, despite weak evidence. Protests filled cities around the world, but both were executed in the electric chair on 23 August 1927. In 1977 the governor of Massachusetts acknowledged that the trial had been unjust.
