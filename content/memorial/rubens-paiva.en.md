An engineer and federal deputy elected in 1962, he lost his seat after the 1964 coup. On 20 January 1971 he was taken from his home in Rio de Janeiro by agents of the dictatorship and killed under torture in an army barracks. His body was never returned to his family. His wife Eunice Paiva's fight to make the state acknowledge the crime is told in the book I'm Still Here, by Marcelo Rubens Paiva, and in Walter Salles's film, which won the Oscar in 2025.
