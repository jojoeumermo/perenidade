An agronomist born in Bafatá, in Guinea when it was a Portuguese colony, to a Cape Verdean family. He founded the PAIGC and led the struggle for the independence of Guinea-Bissau and Cape Verde, combining the liberation war with literacy, health care and the organisation of peasants in the liberated zones. He was assassinated in Conakry on 20 January 1973, months before Guinea-Bissau declared independence.
