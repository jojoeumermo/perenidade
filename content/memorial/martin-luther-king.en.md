A Baptist minister and the foremost leader of the civil rights movement of Black people in the United States. He led the Montgomery bus boycott and the March on Washington, advocated non-violent civil disobedience and received the Nobel Peace Prize in 1964. In his last years he joined the struggle against racism to the struggle against poverty and against the Vietnam War. He was assassinated on 4 April 1968 in Memphis, where he was supporting a strike by sanitation workers.
