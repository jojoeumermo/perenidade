A Swedish immigrant, he worked on the docks, in the mines and on construction sites across the United States, and wrote songs for the Industrial Workers of the World (IWW) that were sung on strikes and picket lines. In 1914 he was accused of a murder in Salt Lake City and convicted in a trial built on weak evidence, which sparked campaigns around the world to save his life. He was executed by firing squad on 19 November 1915. Before he died, he asked his comrades not to waste time mourning, but to organise.
