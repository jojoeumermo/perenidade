Bruno Pereira was an expert on indigenous affairs, one of those who best knew the isolated peoples of the Amazon, and worked with the indigenous organisations of the Javari Valley to guard their lands against illegal fishing, hunting and mining. Dom Phillips, a British journalist based in Brazil, was writing a book on how to save the forest. Both were murdered on 5 June 2022 while travelling by boat on the Itaquaí River. Their deaths showed the world the danger faced by those who defend the Amazon.
