An army captain, he came to power in 1983 in Upper Volta, one of the poorest countries in the world, and gave it a new name: Burkina Faso, "land of upright people". In four years he vaccinated millions of children, promoted literacy campaigns, planted millions of trees against the advance of the desert, banned forced marriage and argued that African countries should not pay the debt inherited from colonialism. He was assassinated on 15 October 1987 in a coup d'état. In 2022 the country's courts convicted those responsible.
