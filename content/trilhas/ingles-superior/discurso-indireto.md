## Contar o que foi dito

- **Discurso direto**: as palavras exatas, entre aspas. *She said, "I am tired."*
- **Discurso indireto**: o relato, sem aspas. *She said (that) she was tired.*

O *that* pode ser omitido na fala.

## O recuo dos tempos

Quando o verbo de relato está no **passado** (*said, told, asked*), os tempos verbais costumam **recuar** um passo:

| Direto | Indireto |
| --- | --- |
| presente simples: "I **work** here." | passado simples: she said she **worked** there |
| presente contínuo: "I **am working**." | passado contínuo: she said she **was working** |
| passado simples: "I **worked**." | past perfect: she said she **had worked** |
| present perfect: "I **have finished**." | past perfect: she said she **had finished** |
| will: "I **will come**." | would: she said she **would come** |
| can: "I **can** help." | could: she said she **could** help |
| must: "I **must** go." | had to: she said she **had to** go |

Se o que foi dito **continua verdadeiro**, o recuo é opcional: *He said the earth **is** round.*

## Outras mudanças

| Direto | Indireto |
| --- | --- |
| I, we | he, she, they |
| this, these | that, those |
| here | there |
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| next week | the following week |
| ago | before |

## Say e tell

- ***say*** + o que foi dito: *She **said** (that) she was late.* Com a pessoa: *say **to** me*.
- ***tell*** + a pessoa + o que foi dito: *She **told me** (that) she was late.*

Nunca *said me*.

## Perguntas e ordens

**Perguntas** viram frases com ordem de afirmação, sem do/did:

- "Where do you work?" → *He asked where I **worked**.*
- "Are you a member?" → *She asked **if** (whether) I was a member.*

**Ordens e pedidos** usam ***tell / ask*** + pessoa + ***to*** + verbo:

- "Wait here." → *He told me **to wait** there.*
- "Please don't smoke." → *She asked us **not to smoke**.*

```verificar
Passe para o indireto: "We will strike tomorrow," the workers said.
- The workers said they will strike tomorrow.
+ The workers said they would strike the next day.
- The workers told they would strike tomorrow.
> will → would; tomorrow → the next day; e "said" sem a pessoa.
```

## Citando autores

Em textos acadêmicos, o discurso indireto aparece o tempo todo, com **verbos de citação** que mostram a postura do autor:

| Verbo | Sentido |
| --- | --- |
| says, states | diz, afirma (neutro) |
| argues, claims | argumenta, defende (tese) |
| suggests | sugere |
| shows, demonstrates | mostra, demonstra (com evidência) |
| points out, notes | observa, assinala |
| criticizes | critica |
| denies | nega |

*Freire **argues** that education is never neutral.* Na escrita acadêmica, esses verbos costumam ficar no **presente**, mesmo para autores já falecidos.

## Para praticar

1. Passe para o indireto: "I live in Fortaleza," she said. / "We have finished the report," they said.
2. Complete com say ou tell: "He ___ me the truth." / "She ___ that she was busy."
3. Passe para o indireto: "Do you speak English?" he asked me. / "Close the door," she told me.
4. Escreva três frases relatando o que um autor que você leu defende, usando argues, shows e suggests.
