## Os que fogem da regra

Muitos dos verbos mais usados do inglês são **irregulares**: o passado não termina em -ed. É preciso aprendê-los um a um, mas a lista dos realmente frequentes não é tão grande, e eles se repetem tanto que logo ficam familiares.

## O passado do to be

O verbo *to be* tem duas formas no passado:

| Pronome | Passado |
| --- | --- |
| I, he, she, it | **was** |
| you, we, they | **were** |

- *I **was** at work.* Eu estava no trabalho.
- *They **were** tired.* Eles estavam cansados.
- Negativa: *I **wasn't**, they **weren't**.*
- Pergunta: ***Were** you at home? **Was** she sick?* (sem did)

Alguns grupos ajudam a memorizar: *buy/bought, bring/brought, think/thought, fight/fought*; *sleep/slept, keep/kept, feel/felt, leave/left*.

## Negativa e pergunta

Exatamente como nos regulares: **did / didn't** e o verbo na forma básica.

- *She **went** to the meeting.* → *She **didn't go**.* → ***Did** she **go**?*
- *They **paid** the rent.* → *They **didn't pay**.* → ***Did** they **pay**?*

```verificar
Complete: "We ___ (meet) at the union last Friday, but we ___ (not have) time to talk."
- meeted / didn't had
+ met / didn't have
- met / didn't had
> Meet é irregular; depois de didn't, forma básica.
```

## Uma história curta

> Yesterday I **woke** up late. I **took** the bus, but it **broke** down. I **got** to work at 9 and my boss **said** nothing. At lunch I **ate** rice and beans with my friends. We **talked** about the new contract. I **left** at 6 and **went** home.

## Para praticar

1. Escreva o passado: go, have, see, make, take, buy, write, think, leave, eat.
2. Complete com was ou were: "I ___ happy." / "They ___ at the meeting." / "___ you at home?"
3. Passe para a negativa: "She bought a new phone." / "We went to the beach."
4. Conte, em seis frases, como foi o seu último fim de semana.
